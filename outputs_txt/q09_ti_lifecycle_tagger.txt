Direction     <- The team defines intelligence requirements and priorities with stakeholders.
Collection    <- Analysts gather indicators from OSINT sources and a honeypot.
Processing    <- Raw logs are parsed and normalized into a common format.
Analysis      <- Analysts correlate indicators and assess attribution of the campaign.
Dissemination <- The team publishes a briefing and shares it with the SOC.
Feedback      <- Stakeholders give feedback so the team can refine future reports.
